A Jira for teams that bill by the hour.

OpsPick keeps the Jira workflow software teams actually use (backlog, board, sprints, reports) and adds what Jira charges extra for: timesheets and a client portal, priced per seat in rupees. This page compares the competition, sets the launch scope, audits the 22 original screens and shows the full redesigned set.

1 · Market

Where OpsPick can win

Jira owns large engineering orgs and Linear owns fast-moving product startups. Neither serves the thousands of Indian IT services companies, agencies and small product teams who need sprints, hour tracking and a way to show clients progress. Today they bolt Tempo onto Jira, or run Zoho Sprints next to spreadsheets.

Built for services teams

Time logging, weekly timesheets with approval, billable export per client, and a guest portal. Jira needs paid add-ons for this; Linear has none of it.

Bought the Indian way

₹ pricing, UPI AutoPay, GST invoices with GSTIN, data hosted in Mumbai. Removes the USD card and forex friction every competitor has.

Easy to switch to

Same vocabulary as Jira (epic, story, sprint, points) and a guided Jira import, so a team moves in an afternoon without retraining.

A mobile app people use

OpsPick started as a mobile app. Triage, comments, status changes and time logging all work well on a phone.

2 · Pricing

Move from per-workspace to per-seat

The original screens priced a whole workspace at ₹199 or ₹499 a month. A 20-person team on Silver would pay about ₹10 per person, and revenue never grows with the customer. Every competitor charges per seat. The new model stays well under Jira and Linear while leaving room for discounts.

3 · Scope

Enough to sell, nothing you'd regret maintaining

The launch list is what a 10–100 person team needs to cancel Jira. The second list ships within roughly three months of launch, and the third is deliberately out.

Launch · v1
  • Email, Google, Apple sign-in · OTP verify · reset · 2FA, passkeys
  • Workspaces, invite links, Owner / Admin / Member / Guest
  • Projects from Scrum, Kanban or Bug-tracking templates
  • Custom statuses, WIP limits, simple automations
  • Epics, stories, tasks, bugs, subtasks, links, labels, points
  • Comments with @mentions, attachments, full history
  • Backlog, board, sprints with start / complete / carry-over
  • Roadmap of epics · list view · filters · saved views · ⌘K
  • Inbox, push, email digest, quiet hours
  • Burndown, velocity, cumulative flow, workload
  • Time logging, timer, weekly timesheet, approvals
  • Client portal for guests
  • GitHub, GitLab, Slack · Jira / CSV import
  • Trial, per-seat plans, Razorpay + UPI AutoPay, GST invoices
  • Data export, audit log, operator console
Next · v1.5
  • Custom fields and a rule builder for automations
  • Public REST API, API tokens, webhooks
  • AI: draft issue from one line, summarise thread, split into subtasks
  • SAML SSO, SCIM, dedicated instance
  • Email-to-issue, Microsoft Teams, Google Calendar
  • Releases / versions and changelog
Not building
  • A JQL-style query language (filters + saved views cover it)
  • An app marketplace
  • Service desk / ITSM queues
  • A wiki or Confluence-style docs
  • Workflow schemes and permission schemes
  • Cross-project portfolio planning
  • Self-managed on-prem installs
4 · Audit

What happened to the original 22 screens

None of the original flows were dropped. One screen was merged, three were redesigned, and the rest were revised in place. The bottom tab bar changed from Home · Issues · Alerts · Settings to Home · Projects · Create · Inbox · More, so creating an issue is one tap from anywhere and admin moves out of the main navigation.
