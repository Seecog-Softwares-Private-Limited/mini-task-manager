Billing questions

Who counts as a paid seat?

Anyone who is a Member, Admin or Owner. Guests (clients, contractors) are free: five per paid seat on Pro and Business.

Do you charge GST?

Yes, 18% GST is added at checkout. Enter your GSTIN to claim input credit; it appears on every invoice.

Which payment methods do you take?

UPI AutoPay, credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex), net banking, and bank transfer against an invoice for yearly plans.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes. Moving to yearly takes effect immediately with credit for the unused month. Moving to monthly takes effect at renewal.

Do you offer discounts?

Registered non-profits and schools get 50% off Business. Early-stage startups in recognised incubators get Pro free for the first year.

What happens to our data if we cancel?

You stay on your plan until the end of the period, then drop to Free. Export everything (CSV plus attachments) from workspace settings at any time.
