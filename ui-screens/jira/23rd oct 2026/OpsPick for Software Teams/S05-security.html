Security

Your work stays in India, and stays private.

How OpsPick stores, protects and lets you control your workspace data.

Hosted in India

Primary data in AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1). Encrypted backups in a second Indian region. Enterprise can choose a dedicated instance.

Encrypted everywhere

TLS 1.2+ in transit. AES-256 at rest for databases, files and backups.

Backups and recovery

Point-in-time recovery for 14 days. Deleted issues and projects stay in Trash for 30 days.

Sign-in controls

2FA, passkeys and Google sign-in on every plan. Enforce 2FA for the workspace. SAML SSO and SCIM on Enterprise.

Roles and access

Owner, Admin, Member and Guest roles. Restrict projects to named people on Business. Guests never see time logs.

Audit log

Who changed permissions, billing, workflows and exports. 90 days on Business, one year on Enterprise.

Your data, your call

Export everything at any time. Delete a workspace and it's gone from production in 14 days and from backups in 35. We process personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
