Questions teams ask first

Is OpsPick a full replacement for Jira?

For most software teams under 200 people, yes: backlog, boards, sprints, epics, roadmap, reports, GitHub and Slack. We deliberately skip Jira's marketplace, service desk and scheme-based permissions. See the comparison page for details.

Where is our data stored?

In India, in Mumbai, with encrypted backups in a second Indian region. Enterprise customers can get a dedicated instance.

How does billing work for Indian companies?

Prices are in rupees. Every invoice is a GST invoice with your GSTIN. Pay by UPI AutoPay, card, net banking or bank transfer against an invoice.

Do clients and contractors need paid seats?

No. Each paid seat includes five guest slots. Guests see only the projects you share and never see time logs or internal comments.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your workspace moves to the Free plan. Nothing is deleted. If you have more than 10 people, you choose who stays active or pick a paid plan.
